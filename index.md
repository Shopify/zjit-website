title: ZJIT

ZJIT is a method-based just-in-time (JIT) compiler for Ruby.
Check out the [source](https://github.com/ruby/ruby/tree/master/zjit)
and [docs](https://docs.ruby-lang.org/en/master/jit/zjit_md.html).
